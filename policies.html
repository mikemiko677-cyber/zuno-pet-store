Here you'll find general information about ZUNO shipping, returns, privacy and website terms.

SHIPPING RETURNS PRIVACY TERMS
01 / SHIPPING

Shipping Policy

ZUNO works to provide customers with clear shipping information before an order is completed.

Processing

Orders may require processing before they are transferred to the appropriate fulfillment or shipping provider.

Delivery estimates

Delivery times can vary depending on the product, destination, fulfillment location, carrier availability and other shipping conditions.

Any delivery estimate shown during checkout should be treated as an estimate rather than a guaranteed delivery date unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Shipping costs

Available shipping methods and costs may vary depending on the customer's delivery destination and the products in the order.

Tracking

When tracking is available, customers may receive tracking information after the order has been processed and shipped.

Incorrect addresses

Customers are responsible for checking their shipping information before submitting an order.

02 / RETURNS

30-Day Return Policy

ZUNO offers a 30-day return window for eligible products.

A return request must be made within 30 days of receiving the product.

Return eligibility

To qualify for a return, the product should generally be returned in the condition in which it was received, with its original components and packaging where reasonably possible.

Products that have been damaged through misuse, modified after delivery, or returned with important components missing may not qualify for a full refund.

Before returning a product

Customers should contact ZUNO before sending a product back.

Do not send a return to an address shown on a package without first receiving the correct return instructions.

Damaged or incorrect products

If an order arrives damaged, defective or materially different from what was ordered, contact ZUNO as soon as reasonably possible and provide the order details and relevant evidence needed to review the issue.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be returned through the applicable payment method where supported.

The time required for a refund to appear can depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

Before launching the store, make sure this return policy matches the actual return terms offered by your suppliers and payment provider.
03 / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

ZUNO may need to process information provided by customers when they browse the website, contact support or place an order.

Information customers provide

Depending on the services enabled on the website, information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and billing information
  • Order information
  • Messages sent to customer support

How information may be used

Information may be used to process orders, provide customer support, deliver products, prevent misuse, operate the website and comply with applicable obligations.

Service providers

Some information may need to be processed by service providers involved in operating the store, such as payment, hosting, shipping or fulfillment providers.

Payment information

ZUNO should use an approved payment provider to handle sensitive payment information.

Customers should never be asked to send complete card details through the ZUNO contact form.

Data requests

Where applicable, customers may contact ZUNO regarding questions or requests concerning their personal information.

04 / TERMS

Terms of Service

These terms describe general conditions for using the ZUNO website and purchasing products through the store.

Website use

Users should use the website only for lawful purposes and should not attempt to interfere with the website, its services or other customers.

Product information

ZUNO aims to present product information clearly. Product appearance, packaging, specifications or other details may change when manufacturers or suppliers update a product.

Prices

Prices displayed on the website may change from time to time.

Any applicable shipping charges, taxes or other costs should be shown during checkout where required and supported by the store's checkout system.

Orders

Submitting an order does not necessarily mean that an order can always be fulfilled.

Orders may need to be cancelled or refunded when, for example, a product is unavailable, a payment cannot be completed, or fulfillment is not available for the destination.

Contact

Questions about these policies can be submitted through the ZUNO contact page.

These policies are a starting template for the store. Before accepting real orders, update them to match your business registration, suppliers, payment provider, countries served and applicable consumer laws.
